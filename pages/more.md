# Vowel Extensions

 - `aia` - between `ah` and `ia` (IPA: ɐ)
 - `eae` - between `eh` and `ea` (IPA: e̞ —OR— ɛ̝)
 - `iau` - between `ao` and `au` (IPA: ɐʊ)
 - `aeu` - between `eo` and `eu` (IPA: e̞ʊ —OR— ɛ̝ʊ)
 - `iay` - between `ai` and `ay` (IPA: ɐɪ)
 - `aey` - between `ei` and `ey` (IPA: e̞ɪ —OR— ɛ̝ɪ)
 - `ioy` - between `oi` and `oy` (IPA: oɪ)
